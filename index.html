01 / Focus

Making complex clinical language legible to machines.

My work focuses on information extraction from clinical documents in multilingual settings. I am interested in robust language models that can help transform free-text medical data into structured information for research and care.

A

Multilingual information extraction

Methods that respect the linguistic variety found in real-world clinical documentation.

B

Large language models

Studying capable, trustworthy models for specialised natural language understanding tasks.

C

Medical data applications

Connecting NLP research to questions that matter in clinical and biomedical settings.

02 / Context

Research grounded in data, language, and people.

I am a PhD student in Brain, Mind and Computer Science at Fondazione Bruno Kessler, in partnership with the University of Padua. My project is supervised by Alberto Lavelli and investigates multilingual information extraction from clinical documents.

Before the PhD, I earned a BSc in Statistics at the University of Padua and an MSc in Big Data Management and Analytics through a joint programme at ULB, UPC, and the University of Padua.
