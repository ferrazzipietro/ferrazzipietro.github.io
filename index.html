About

Researching language technologies for clinical data.

My PhD project, supervised by Alberto Lavelli, investigates information extraction from clinical documents in multilingual settings. I am interested in reliable language models that help turn unstructured medical text into usable data for research and care.

I hold a BSc in Statistics from the University of Padua and an MSc in Big Data Management and Analytics delivered jointly by Universite libre de Bruxelles, Universitat Politecnica de Catalunya, and the University of Padua.
